Eval Set Review: drug-interaction-check

Current description: Answers questions about interactions between drugs (drug-drug interactions, DDI) and between a drug and other bioactive compounds such as foods, beverages, alcohol, herbal medicines, supplements, and natural products. Use whenever someone asks whether medicines can be taken together, whether a drug is safe with coffee, grapefruit, alcohol, herbs, vitamins, or supplements, what happens when two drugs are combined, whether a combination changes drug levels or causes a side effect, CYP450 or P-glycoprotein interactions, or wants a prescription, medication list, or supplement stack screened. Trigger even without the word "interaction", e.g. "can I take X with Y?" or "is this combo okay for my patient?". Grounded in an i3L book on DDI in Indonesian clinical settings, but specific interaction facts come from labelled or live sources, not the book. Not for single-drug dosing or general pharmacology with no combination involved.
